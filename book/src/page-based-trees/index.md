# Page-based trees

The B-tree family: balanced trees of fixed-size pages with a high fan-out, updated in place. They
are the default index of relational databases. The variants in this chapter change how pages are
updated, to allow more concurrency or faster writes.

- [Bε-tree](b-epsilon-tree.md)
- [Bw-tree](bw-tree.md)
